How to use

  1. Choose a conversion direction using the toggle at the top
  2. Type or paste content into the left panel
  3. The right panel updates in real time with the converted output
  4. Use Copy or Download to export the result

About

Inkdown is a standalone, privacy-focused converter that runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any external server, and it works offline after the initial page load.
